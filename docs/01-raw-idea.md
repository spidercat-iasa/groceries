# Step 1 — The raw idea

*Written by hand, with no AI involved. This is the starting point for everything that follows.*

---

**Goal** - provide members of a household or small tightly knit groups, a tool to manage a shared shopping list.

**Group experience** - all group members can access and update lists created by the admin. Group can have any number of shared lists. Group has separate space for managing recipes. Group member can add or remove individual items to/from a shopping list, or add a group of items from chosen recipe to shopping list of his desires.
