# Product 1-Pager: Household Grocery & Recipe Hub (Working Title)

## 1. Target Persona & Context

**Primary Persona:** Multi-person households (parents and older kids, or roommates sharing groceries) who cook regularly at home and split grocery planning/shopping—specifically modeled on a household where one or two "grocery captains" coordinate lists while everyone contributes meal ideas and items.

**Distribution Scope:** Purpose-built as personal, high-craft software for immediate household use (and future independent living), with potential App Store release strictly secondary.

## 2. The "Hair-on-Fire" Problem (JTBD)

**Job to be Done:** "When our household is planning meals and restocking groceries for the week, we want a single shared space to store our go-to recipes and push their ingredients straight onto our active shopping list, so we stop losing links in chat threads and stop acting as manual copy-paste translators before every store run."

**Current Breakdown:** Recipe links are buried across abandoned boards (Trello) and messaging threads (family/personal chats). Turning a planned meal into a Bring! list requires manual ingredient-by-ingredient entry by whoever runs the shopping list.

## 3. Core Value Proposition

A lightweight, shared household utility that bridges private recipe storage and real-time collaborative shopping lists—turning saved meal links or text recipes into categorized shopping list items in one tap.

## 4. Primary Differentiator

**Frictionless "Link-to-List" Speed with Zero Bloat:** Lighter and faster than heavy recipe managers (like Paprika), yet smarter than standalone list apps (like Bring! or Google Keep) because recipes live right next to the lists and push ingredients directly into them.

## 5. Explicit Non-Goals (V1)

- **No In-App Recipe Search or Discovery Feed:** Users import recipes they already found elsewhere; this is a private vault, not Pinterest.
- **No Automated Pantry/Fridge Inventory:** Avoids manual stock tracking that users abandon and stop trusting.
- **No Supermarket / Delivery API Integrations:** Stays fast, store-agnostic, and free of regional lock-in.
- **No Physical Cookbook Photo/OCR Scanning (V1):** Focuses strictly on URL scraping and raw-text paste first.
- **No App Store Growth/Monetization Mechanics:** Zero onboarding funnels, paywalls, or social sharing features in V1.
